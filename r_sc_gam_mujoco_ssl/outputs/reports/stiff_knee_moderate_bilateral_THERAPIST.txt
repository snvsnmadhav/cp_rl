R-SC-GAM-MJ-SSL produces: muscle recruitment likelihood, estimated actuator
stress, estimated muscle-tendon strain, joint torque burden, mechanical loading
index, and simulation-based hypothesis generation.
It does NOT measure EMG, does NOT confirm tissue stress, does NOT diagnose CP,
and does NOT prescribe treatment. Every output must be interpreted by a qualified
clinician alongside instrumented 3D gait analysis, dynamic EMG, and physical
examination.
