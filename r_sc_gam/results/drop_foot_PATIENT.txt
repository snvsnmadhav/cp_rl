YOUR WALKING PATTERN SUMMARY  --  closest match: Drop Foot
----------------------------------------------------------------
Your walking pattern most closely resembles a recognised pattern
called 'Drop Foot'. In plain terms: insufficient swing-phase
dorsiflexion (foot drop) with intact passive ankle rom; weak /
poorly-timed dorsiflexors rather than spastic equinus.
compensatory hip & knee flexion to clear foot.

What this means for your therapy focus:
  - Tibialis anterior: is weak -> focus on strengthening it
  - Iliopsoas: just needs monitoring
  - Hamstrings: just needs monitoring

These are general, evidence-based directions. Your therapist or
doctor will tailor the exact exercises, braces or treatments to
you after an in-person assessment.
