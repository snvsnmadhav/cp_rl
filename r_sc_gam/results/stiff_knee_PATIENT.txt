YOUR WALKING PATTERN SUMMARY  --  closest match: Stiff-Knee Gait
----------------------------------------------------------------
Your walking pattern most closely resembles a recognised pattern
called 'Stiff-Knee Gait'. In plain terms: reduced and delayed
peak knee flexion in swing from rectus femoris co-spasticity,
impairing foot clearance.

What this means for your therapy focus:
  - Rectus femoris: is working too hard / too tight -> focus on stretching & tone relief
  - Soleus: just needs monitoring
  - Gastrocnemius: just needs monitoring

These are general, evidence-based directions. Your therapist or
doctor will tailor the exact exercises, braces or treatments to
you after an in-person assessment.
