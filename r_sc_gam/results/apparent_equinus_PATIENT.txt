YOUR WALKING PATTERN SUMMARY  --  closest match: Apparent Equinus
----------------------------------------------------------------
Your walking pattern most closely resembles a recognised pattern
called 'Apparent Equinus'. In plain terms: toe-walking
appearance with normal ankle dorsiflexion rom; the apparent
equinus is produced by excessive hip and knee flexion in stance.
treating the ankle here is a classic error.

What this means for your therapy focus:
  - Hamstrings: is working too hard / too tight -> focus on stretching & tone relief
  - Iliopsoas: is working too hard / too tight -> focus on stretching & tone relief
  - Gluteus maximus: is weak -> focus on strengthening it

These are general, evidence-based directions. Your therapist or
doctor will tailor the exact exercises, braces or treatments to
you after an in-person assessment.
