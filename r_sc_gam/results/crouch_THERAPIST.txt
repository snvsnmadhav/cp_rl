-- Intervention rationale (top 3) -------------------------------------------
   * Hamstrings (Spasticity management):
       Velocity-dependent over-activity dominates. Stretching / serial
       casting / focal BoNT-A; if multi-level & severe consider SDR or
       tone-reducing surgery. Pair with antagonist strengthening +
       orthosis.
   * Iliopsoas (Spasticity management):
       Velocity-dependent over-activity dominates. Stretching / serial
       casting / focal BoNT-A; if multi-level & severe consider SDR or
       tone-reducing surgery. Pair with antagonist strengthening +
       orthosis.
   * Gluteus maximus (Strengthening / motor control):
       Weakness / poor timing dominates. Progressive resistance training,
       FES or task-specific motor training; orthosis for compensation
       while strength is rebuilt.

-- Archetype clinical note ---------------------------------------------------
   Quadriceps overload + plantarflexor/knee-extension lever-arm failure.
   Strengthen quads & plantarflexors, hamstring lengthening, GRAFO
   orthoses; surgical correction of lever-arm deformities. Avoid further
   heel-cord lengthening.
